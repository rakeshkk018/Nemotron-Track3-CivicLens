The Problem

80% of citizens cannot effectively read government documents — dense legal language, 40+ page PDFs, missed deadlines, and zero plain-English explanations leave people in the dark about decisions that directly affect their lives.

CivicLens AI reads any government PDF and extracts what matters in seconds — plain-language summaries, action deadlines, who is affected, and source-cited AI answers. Supports English, ಕನ್ನಡ, हिंदी & தமிழ்.

The Impact

Every citizen — students, seniors, NGOs, journalists, lawyers — gains equal access to civic intelligence. No more missed deadlines, no more confusion, no more democratic exclusion by complexity.
